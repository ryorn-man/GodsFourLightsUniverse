## Is Refusing a Drug Craving a Form of Meditation?
*Author: Vesper Ashfield*

Observing a drug craving without acting on it can provide great insight into the tricks and traps of the mind. You are larger than your thoughts in Layer 1, or your visual field in Layer 4. You are larger than any negative emotions arising, or the vortex of thinking which guides toward a relapse. An addiction can provide a visceral source of death-contemplation (maranasati),[^1] which is recommended in Buddhism for reaching enlightenment. If you acquire a wide array of coping strategies to avoid active addiction, and implement them enthusiastically,[^2] this will easily overlap with methods for reaching Fundamental Wellbeing. Methods include: practicing seated meditation, reading spiritual books, going to 12-Step meetings, filling out self-inquiry worksheets, attending other recovery programs, writing gratitude lists, and immersing oneself deeply in exercise or creative practices.

I may not always know what I should do or where I should go to ascend further into Location Three or Location Five of the Path of Humanity, where there is a sense of union with a divine or panpsychic intelligence, but I always know where to find the accursed Void Lantern of Location Five of the Path of Freedom: my mind will summon this low-emotion state if I choose to go on a binge of dextromethorphan-containing cough medicine. (“Void Lantern” is my own terminology).

Layers and Locations:[^3]
> **Terminology** According to *The Layers of Depth in Fundamental Wellbeing* by Annemiek Meyer and Jeffery Martin, the Layers are always present in the nervous system, while the Locations are unlocked through brain changes. The Layers are much better-known across traditions than the Locations. The system documented by Martin and Meyer divides the Path of Humanity and Path of Freedom from each other beginning at Location Five. **Caution: Layer 4 can be extremely reinforcing and difficult to escape once a person heavily sinks in, especially in Location 5+ of the Path of Freedom, where serious functional impairment is common. This may well be a factor in many drug addictions and other compulsive behaviors, though more research is needed.**
> My system instead divides them from the beginning of the continuum, recognizing the differentiation between high-emotion and low-emotion areas, and also the ability of faint glimpses of Location 5 (such as from my morphine and ketamine experience described later here) to color earlier Locations.

My addiction knows the nihilistic cold of the Path of Freedom is useful for acquiring DXM, because it bypasses my self-preservation instincts. In turn, the bottles of Robitussin[^4] and Delsym from CVS or Wal-Tussin from Walgreens reliably summon both light and shadow—but my addiction knows how to steer into the frozen sea.

It is very likely everyone is capable of accessing the benefits of persistent Awakening / Fundamental Wellbeing, including: increased present-moment awareness, a constant sense of peace, increased mental energy, decreased neuroticism, and decreased cravings for addictive stimuli.[^5] This is consistent with Buddhist doctrine,[^6] and also with the fellowships of Alcoholics Anonymous[^7] and Narcotics Anonymous. Contrary to Buddhist doctrine, however, it is possible to lose ongoing states of Fundamental Wellbeing, usually from excessive stress and usually before the seven-month mark.[^8] If others use hallucinogens to amplify meditation, I would hate for them to lose their progress due to the harsh trials of addiction.

Along with Benadryl, DXM was one of the first drugs my system encountered in life. Many times when I had a bad cough growing up, my parents made me drink Delsym 12 Hour Cough Relief. I hated the flavor, and do not remember consciously enjoying the effects of those very low doses (by recreational standards). It is very likely, however, that the compound provided both antidepressant and stimulant effects. Maybe even shallow glimpses of Awakening / Fundamental Wellbeing.

Later, at age thirteen, I dislocated my shoulder and needed it fixed in the hospital. The staff gave me general anesthetic levels of ketamine, which completely[^9] eliminated any pain and any memory of the shoulder reduction procedure. No doubt the morphine, Ativan, and promethazine from earlier that evening potentiated the ketamine. I spent what felt like several hours afterward in a profound dissociative state, hallucinating strange things and forgetting where I was.

A mixture of euphoria and frustration, anxiety relief and anxiety-generation, rolled through my nervous system while the ketamine wore off. I saw plants growing on the wall, plants with the face of the Starship Mario from *Super Mario Galaxy 2*. It is very likely I overestimated how long I was in the hospital, because of “time dilation” from ketamine. It also killed the majority of my depression for a week or so afterward.

Alcoholism runs in my family. Like ketamine, both DXM and alcohol are NMDA antagonists, though alcohol is not suitable for use as a hallucinogen because it has a very non-selective mechanism of action. When I started abusing DXM in adulthood, on the other hand, it brought back the warm, stupefying mystical soup from that night in the hospital. I quickly developed a harmful pattern of using high doses a few times a month, which interfered with my school work, and created numerous paranoid episodes. There was intense euphoria from taking DXM and meditating, with were moments of empathy and spiritual insight. There was also the blind craving for oblivion. I went to rehab eventually after getting into conflict with my family during a miniature psychotic break, and I quit DXM until I moved out from my parents’ houses into an apartment 1.5 years later.

When I resumed using DXM in my first apartment, I rationalized that it would help me use meditation to reach enlightenment, which would help me stay away from drugs and other harmful activities. This was a great example of the dangers of half-knowledge. While DXM did facilitate an array of positive spiritual states, and a lot of writing, it also caused seizures, extreme amounts of anger, and other deleterious effects. My Wellbutrin and Strattera massively potentiated the DXM by inhibiting CYP2D6 and doing other unfortunate things.[^10] I further boosted the effects by combining it with weed and/or classical psychedelics. My DXM tolerance and my DXM use escalated until I felt utterly unable to stay away from it for more than a few days, and until those days did almost nothing to reduce my tolerance. My bank account balance sank below zero. I started skipping work a lot. I got into many verbal fights with my family members. 

When my DXM use helped me enter Location 1 of Fundamental Wellbeing—corresponding roughly to stream-entry from Theravada Buddhism, or Kenshō from Zen Buddhism—for a few months, it became easier not to take it every single day, yet my overall mentality got even worse. An immense amount of buried trauma began surfacing, while my ego and the negative structural dissociation in my system utilized the constant background peace of Location 1 to ignore any and all social disapproval to continue getting high. I had strange and frightening paranormal experiences. Laundry lay unfolded on my floor for days on end. Bugs infested my apartment. I encountered a level of functional degradation beyond even my days living in my parents’ two homes. I crashed the car my father had given me. My DXM use finally ended when I went to a psych ward after going psychotic on an old friend, and then visited a six-month rehab program full of other narcissistic and sociopathic males, where the stress knocked me out of Location 1 and back into normal consciousness.

There have been later instances of DXM use. I am clean from all drugs today, and am persistently in Location 1 or beyond. The first time I got strongly high from DXM, I knew it had the potential to kill me, and I know my life will be shortened dramatically if I go back to using it. Many times while DXM tripping, I sensed the presence of absolute evil and spitefulness, and other people behaved in strange ways around me, both good and bad. The sense of evil may have been my mind’s delusional interpretation of the unattached burdens and legacy burdens fueling my destructive behaviors, or it may have been my mind’s delusional translation of actual evil spirits in the vicinity, if the unattached burdens and the evil spirits are even distinct from each other at all. Any insights offered by the DXM were greatly hampered by the schizophrenia-like distortions of reality it causes.

While I feel immense guilt for many of my actions on DXM, I am grateful for the fact that observing the cravings without attaching to them can quickly guide me toward the light. The ability of dextromethorphan to alleviate depression, and even generate temporary Fundamental Wellbeing, can be extremely enticing for people who feel the way I typically felt back then. 

In my experience, Fundamental Wellbeing confers resistance to narcissistic abuse through a variety of mechanisms, including providing distance from one’s thoughts, decreasing the need for approval, and making PTSD triggers get weaker and weaker each time they’re activated. Thank God for the brief glimpses of Location 3 during those binges, which provided me with sometimes-agonizing insights into my own dysfunction, and also proved to me other ways of life were possible.

I hope everyone in the world learns to access these states through healthy and safe ways, if these states benefit them. Dissociatives are like taking a blowtorch to a spiderweb, and while I’m sure many people can have better experiences from dissociative drugs than me, they are not suited for 24-hour functioning.
