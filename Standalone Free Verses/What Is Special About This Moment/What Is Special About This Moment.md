# What Is Special About This Moment?
*Author: Vesper Ashfield*

Today on October 12th I am working on inquiries from a 12-Step fellowship! And worksheets for Internal Family Systems! I am grateful to have discovered the gift of persistent high-emotion Awakening / Fundamental Wellbeing, which far surpasses any temporary pleasures I have experienced in life, and I wish to use this new power to build new loops of behavior and thinking! Let thinking become a servant of the Fullness, rather than a tyrant!

Today, the Pollard Family in Texas sold a 650-acre property to become a state park. *“This land is part of my soul,” said Sam Pollard.* The family has owned this land for 80 years, farming pecans, and now wishes to leave a legacy of conservation behind. It is filled with pinewoods, wetlands, and a pecan orchard, and it is the longest remaining contiguous piece of land running along Lake Palestine![^1] The Texas Tribune says the Pollard family has owned the land since 1940, placing the duration longer at around 86 years.[^2]

Back on August 4, 2026, The Phoenix Species Project launched a global effort—led by the Bezos Earth Fund and Leonardo DiCaprio’s Re:wild; bringing together more than 100 local and international partners from governments, Indigenous communities, local communities, and conservationist forces—to save 100 species threatened by extinction! These species include: the bowmouth guitarfish, the night parrot, the Hickory Nut Gorge green salamander, and the succulent plant called the fuzzy belly button![^3]

Today, a cyclist named Gary Shaughnessy and his team completed a 1,500-kilometer charity bike ride from Switzerland to the Isle of Man! Diagnosed with Parkinson’s disease in 2015, Gary uses endurance sports to raise funds and awareness, and to manage his symptoms.[^4]

Over in Australia, the NASA-USGS Landsat 8 satellite photographed a massive wildflower bloom in the Murchison region of the outback in August this year. ScienceDaily republished the information today in an article. The images collected by the OLI (Operational Land Imager) on the satellite were made possible by heavy rains from cold fronts, allowing flowers to grow in what may be the largest bloom in 20 years![^5]

Past October 12th events: The (1850) opening[^6] of the Female Medical College of Pennsylvania (later renamed the Woman’s Medical College of Pennsylvania), the first medical school in the world that granted women the M.D. degree![^7] Desmond Thomas Doss earning the Medal of Honor in 1945 for his bravery in saving 75 men while under fire from the Japanese, making Doss the first conscientious objector to win this award![^8] The Soviet Union’s (1964) launch of Voskhod 1, the first space vehicle with more than one person (it had three) onboard![^9] The (1999) birth of the six billionth living human, estimated by the United Nations![^10]
