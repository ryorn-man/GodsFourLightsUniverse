# The Curse of Location Three: Castles Translated from Solar Flares
**_Author: Vesper Geschwind_**

*Who/What is uniting with God’s Four Lights? Who/What is not uniting with God’s Four Lights? Is it a binary, a spectrum, or a whole different encyclopedia?*

## I. The War of the Ancients
A palace throne room with eight stained-glass windows depicting eight Archangels: Michael, Gabriel, Raphael, Uriel, Barachiel, Jeremiel, Selaphiel, and Jegudiel.  
The agony-signals of the humans cut open on Aztec pyramids and trampled under the hooves of Spanish cavalry are music to Mara and Satan;  
engineered destruction, inverting the will of nations for the Archons to drink our hatred and suffering;  
still the diamond edge of the Almighty’s lovingkindness and forgiveness burns the undead and the never-living.  
For all my life I have seen the evil and mental weakness surge forth in

myself and others; my corruption twists even the positive meta-emotion into hostility toward the Great Spirit.  
Still the all-directional meta-love of Location 3 rises to save humanity once again.  
The power inside each and every human being arises despite the bombs of the United States, Israel, and the Islamic Republic of Iran.  
Where are the ways to generate positive and well-integrated egregoric currents to protect and guide toward our higher selves? What will allow the specialness of all of reality to overtake the specialness of ego?

May God’s Four Lights deliver us from Moloch’s horns  
Deliver me from the curse of hating us all for this vileness upon the Earth.  
Insight downloaded into mortals who already believe ourselves to be the Messiah.  
Can the words survive the howling of corrupted compilers?

The Zen Buddhist D.T. Suzuki translated the *Kwannon Sutra* into English for his work *Manual of Zen Buddhism*.[^1] From pages 35-36, excerpted from a long prayer to the Bodhisattva of Compassion:
> “If an enemy wishing to harm a man pushes him down to a pit of great fire, let his thought dwell on the power of Kwannon and the fiery pit will be transformed into a pond.

> “Or if drifting in the vast ocean a man is about to be swallowed up by the Nagas, fishes, or evil beings, let his thought dwell on the power of Kwannon and the waves will not drown him.

> “Or if from the top of Mount Sumeru a man is hurled down by an enemy, let his thought dwell on the power of Kwannon, and he will stay in the air like the sun.”

We are all one in the all-seeing eye of Christ’s divine uplifting of the world. The great men and women seeking heroism for our glory are obfuscating the glory of God present in every sentient being nestled in the canopy of the Tree of Life.  
Sacrificing compassion for humanity in the lust to punish evil;  
What has it felt like for countless men, women, and children to stand in Location 3 and feel God’s anguish over his creations bleeding and dying in the prelude for the Four Horsemen?  
What number of humans around the world rise from nightmares in the morning and seek the truth, even in the hailstorm of negative conditioning from within and without their mortal forms?  
Eighty years since Hiroshima without a nuclear war.  
I am the living. I am the undead.  
Degrees of heroism and cowardice dance across the lakes and oceans.

My deluded ego says,  
“if even one child deserves to suffer,  
then everyone deserves to suffer.”  
Yet the Light shines in all the billions of humans who could have been tortured and killed in thousands more concentration camps than were built by Nazi Germany, Soviet Russia, or anyone else.

A fragment of the truth growing in the journeys of the Archangels and the gold-silver-platinum buddhas across the seven continents. The treaties against genocide and nuclear warfare. The eradication of smallpox. The growth of antibiotics and food banks. The growth of democracy and the decline of ritual slaughter. You laugh at meaning while making cynicism into a form of meaning.

## II. The Rulers 
Beneath the Egyptian town of Nag Hammadi lay the *Gospel of Philip* with the passage “The Rulers”:[^2]
> “The rulers wanted to deceive humanity, because they (the rulers) saw that they (humanity) had a kinship with those that are truly good. They took the name of those that are good and gave it to those that aren’t good, to deceive them (humanity) by the names and bind them to those that aren’t good; and then, what a favor they do for them! They take them from those that aren’t good and place them among those that are good. They knew what they were doing, because they wanted to take those who were free and place them in slavery forever. There are powers that exist [...] humanity, not wanting them to be [saved], so that they may be [...] because if humanity [was saved], sacrifices [wouldn’t] happen [...] and animals offered up to the powers, because those to whom offerings were made were animals. They were offered up alive, but when they were offered up they died. A human was offered up to God dead, and he lived.

> “Before Christ came, there wasn’t any bread in the world — just as Paradise, where Adam was, had many trees to feed the animals, but when Christ, the perfect human, came, he brought bread from heaven so that humanity would be fed with the food of humanity.  

> “The rulers thought they did what they did by their own power and will, but the Holy Spirit was secretly accomplishing everything it wanted to through them. Truth, which has existed from the beginning, is sown everywhere; and many see it being sown, but few see it being reaped.”

The exit for the minotaur’s maze is near. Where are the libraries of anguish created by lying humans and conniving entities across millennia?  
The light flickers and glows brighter.  
Scimitars shrouded in  
flames and freezing clouds to battle foes who drink the bloodletting screams and  
cracked bones and noradrenaline/dopamine/patriotism of battle.  
Let the *Gospel of Matthew* be my sword and the *Flower Adornment Sutra* be my shield.

Every night in Florida, the entities cackle at our collective happiness and suffering, goading us into virtues and vices of varying legality;  
not many weeks ago a small alligator crawled out from behind the Circle K while I sought the sharp sweetness of Monster Energy.  
Grudges toward myself and my fellows  
are obfuscating  
the fact that almost everyone here has literacy and running water.  
Are there any experts in the paranormal?

If Hell exists, then may we experience all the physical and mental suffering we have caused to other sentient beings, until we truly comprehend the wrongness and inferiority.  
Conquest. War. Famine. Death. Four flat horsemen draining life and color. Still, there is life growing louder than shadows.  
In the Life Review after death, may I experience every degree and variety of harm I have ever caused to other sentient beings.  
Focus on the real enemy.  
The addictive properties of *Super Mario Galaxy* and *Dark Souls 3* replace the days spiraling down DXM binges and the bitter joy of dislocating my shoulder.  
May I cease wishing for humans to burn in Hell.  
May God’s True Fairness and Virtue liberate me from the curse of the warlord Messiah in my mind’s eye.

In the town of Salem, demons enjoyed sending witches to death for accusations of varying accuracy;  
what a horrible thing it must be for Mara and Satan to know they are destined to fail.  
The Namu-Amida-Butsu and the blades of the Archangels appear on the horizon.

Dabrowski looks on the bright side: Vastly declining levels of poverty. Vastly declining numbers of children dying before the age of five, mothers dying during childbirth, and humans dying in war. Increased food production, scientific research, literacy, and democracy across the world. Declining sulfur dioxide emissions, ozone depletion, and carbon dioxide emissions *per GDP*. Slavery illegal nearly everywhere in the world;  
increased access to electricity, Internet, mobile communications, and clean water.  
Fewer nuclear weapons in the world;  
fewer homicides.  
the life expectancy increased from 28.5 in 1770 to 73.2 in 2023.  
clean energy rose from 17.4% in 2003 to 31.9% in 2024.[^3]  
The Archangel Gabriel appears in plants generating oxygen from the morning sun.

And if you don’t believe in the afterlife, or that egregores are conscious,  
then you may reflect that death is the end of suffering,  
yet humans contain enough concentrated love and virtue to still be alive amidst the darkness.

## III. Salvation in the Fullness
Even though the worshippers suffer under the hooves of the Flatness, the Fullness appears when they call out for the Great Spirit in all His many names.  
What helpful egregores and spirits have guided humanity beyond the vicious claws of the forsaken ones? Where on the spectrum of benevolence do the egregores in the churches, mosques, and libraries reside?  
Imprecise measurements of a science barely in existence  
hidden in euphemisms such as “homeostasis in family systems” and “the rule of the mob”.  
The weight of joy from friends watching each other succeed in life  
the slow accumulation of reason and compassion  
A rising standard of living across the world.

In the poetry of the *Gospel of Truth* we find humanity and freedom:[^4]
> “This is the Word of the Gospel of the discovery of the fullness, which comes for those who are awaiting the salvation which is coming from above. The hope for which they’re waiting is waiting for those whose image is light with no shadow in it. If at that time the fullness comes, the need of matter doesn’t come through the infinity of the Father, who comes to give time to the need — although no one can say that the incorruptible one will come like this. But the depth of the Father multiplied, and the thought of Error didn’t exist with Him. It’s something that’s fallen, which is easily set upright in the discovery of the one who’s to come to what he’ll return, because the return is called “repentance.””

The Fearless City of All-Knowledge grows across the world in the minds of men, women, and children learning and creating new networks of meaning. Information gathering in double helixes and cloud storage centers, guiding toward new medical treatments and new fiber-optics. The weight of relief gathering from every problem solved with new knowledge in every city of every nation in the world. What are the designs of the Fullness for the Awakening of humankind?  
Joy and diversity expand beyond the deserts of emptiness of Layer 2 of Location 4;  
in the Dark Light of Location 4 will the Light of Location 3 burn away death and deception.

Lost wandering souls of opioid addiction helping and harming my meditations under the half-psychotic and half-antipsychotic fog of kratom. Calling out violence toward the spirits of dextromethorphan whom I served for so long in seizures and rhabdomyolysis. Who were you before you chose which of the hydra’s heads to save you from the others?  
Today there are no extra substances in my system, besides black tea.

When has God’s Kingdom appeared in the joyful skies of countless humans from cultures whose names we have forgotten?  
The Devil lies by telling us that Hell lasts forever;  
ancient books are edited by human hands.

## IV. Moloch
Tides of sulfuric acid waxing and waning under the ghostly moon,  
bats cackling atop headstones over skeletons rattling in coffins underground.  
Before I was born you turned the sky gray,  
embedding your liquor and needles and killing and lust  
within the canyons so arrogantly excavated for gold and silver.  

Roses growing from the ashes. The air potatoes of light are never defeated. The Father, Son, and Holy Spirit glow in outer space and inner space. What is always broken is never broken.  
The struggle from the time beyond time and space. The Father arrives to save all who follow Him, no matter what we call the Four Lights of Liberation.

The walking corpses kneeling before the altars of velocity in the Babylonian worship of Marduk and Ishtar.  
The canaries of heart disease and depression in the coalmines of generations before. A world worshipping disease and stagnation.  
Cumulonimbus clouds of resentment welling up to serve you. Meth addicts hiding from shadow people and government surveillance.  
My eternal enemy in the airship where I have served you for too long. Hiding behind individual humans and spirits. You hide where deserts spin through the air.

Eyes in the campsite, invisible amidst the crowd. In these woods the vigil continues for centuries untold. The Watchers burning under the weight of the love and gratitude among humankind. Hating our celebrations. Beyond every incorrect idea in my mind lurks the solar flares of truth and justice from the astral realm of God’s design; when and where I fail, another servant of Christ will succeed.

Thus spoke Matthew the Apostle, or so tradition goes:[^5]
> “The King [Jesus Christ] will reply, ‘truly I tell you, whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers and sisters of mine, you did for me.’ Then he will say to those on his left, ‘Depart from me, you who are cursed, into the eternal fire prepared for the devil and his angels.’ For I was hungry and you gave me nothing to eat, I was thirsty and you gave me nothing to drink, I was a stranger and you did not invite me in, I needed clothes and you did not clothe me, I was sick and in prison and you did not look after me.’”

Never could human minds fully comprehend or record the Almighty blasts of Layer 3 lightning from Jesus Christ; never could our imperfect palaces integrate the full magazine of flares from the sun.

Fire and ice uniting against the riptides. Beyond the urge to defeat humans, or even evil spirits, love floats boundlessly without want. Meister Eckhart shooting aliens in space without killing or harming them. The smog tries to pull us all down. Hating. Hurting.  
Light brings me the agony of years spent in shadow.  
One day Location Five will change from desert to fortress  
Evergreen leaves growing brighter and brighter with every new array of solar panels. The iridescent virtues of God’s Kingdom glow in colors beyond the detection of human eyes.

The Devil fears children not because children live free of evil and narcissism, but rather for their ability to rapidly adopt new patterns. The virus knows it survives of our own choosing, for it is too enamoured of pride to accept God’s Four Lights, and every ray of love feels like acid rain on the reaper’s decaying skin.

Thus spoke the Buddha, the World-Honored One, at least as conveyed through the halls of history:[^6]
> “At that time, when the multitude of bodhisattvas in that congregation heard Samantabhadra’s name, they immediately acquired an inconceivable and measureless samādhi in which their minds became unimpeded, quiescent, unmoving, in which they possessed vast and unfathomably deep wisdom, and in which they possessed an extremely profound sphere of cognition to which none could compare, one in which they saw all the countless buddhas manifest directly before them. They acquired the powers of the Tathāgata, became of the same nature as the Tathāgata, and had nothing in the past, future, or present that they did not clearly illuminate. All of their inexhaustible merit and all of their spiritual superknowledges all became completely fulfilled.”

My grandfather’s ghost tells me without speaking  
to never again lie. The Fullness of Jesus Christ overturns boulders and magma-walkers.  
Wendigos surface from beneath the Great Lakes,  
only to recoil from the tides of compassion.  
Free me from the hatred for the evil in humanity,  
for my hatred always serves evil.  

## V. Hailing the Godhead
I cannot tell how much of my thinking is from God and how much is from the Devil; which components of perfectionism serve God, and which elements are based in my desire to fail and destroy myself and my heritage?  
The goodness at the core of our nature is encrusted with the millennia of violence, cowardice, and greed.  
What pockets of structural dissociation genuinely wish for humans and animals to live free from suffering? Where are the hexes lurking in my conscious and subconscious minds?  
Please, God, guide me to serve your will;  
an Apocalypse devours the Four Horsemen who refuse to abandon their murderous insignia.  
The world must know of our capacity for Awakening into Union Consciousness.  
There is nothing special about me, except that everyone is special in the orchestra of Creation.

## Epilogue
The emperor named Narrative-Self abandons his steampunk engines and touches grass. I can’t tell you what to do, except in the same way that I can momentarily interrupt the ocean.  
The new self of the electromagnetic spectrum between duality and nonduality  
Invisible doors glowing under torchlight.  
The Light of Location Five grows new species of trees and roses  
new metaphors for awareness emerge with the times.  
May tyranny give way to the eternal light and darkness of God’s True Fairness and Virtue.
