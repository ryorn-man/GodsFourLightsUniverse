# What/Who/When/Where is God’s Dark Light of Location 4?
*(Author: Vesper Geschwind)*

Truthfully, I am an expert neither in spirituality nor in neuroscience; it is all I can do to keep my sailboat from being devoured by krakens in the morning.

Basic properties of Location Four of Awakening / Fundamental Wellbeing include: even deeper inner peace and freedom than earlier Locations, a loss of emotion, a loss of agency, memory problems, a profound loss of dualistic self-other perceptions, a lack of regard for social approval, and a sense of alienness to one’s existence.[^1] In my opinion for my life, Location Four is an amazing component of a colorful toolbox for navigating awareness, yet a terrible place to make a home. Others are free to specialize in low-emotion states.

While these four quotes may not entirely succeed at distinguishing the Dark Light of Location 4 from the accursed Void Lantern of Location 4, they are certainly an improvement over my smog-filled goggles of the past and present. What will guide me to use the gift of Geschwind Syndrome to generate positive egregoric currents?

What is the literal accuracy of our holy books? Are they best interpreted like giant Zen koans? Who were Christ and Buddha at the epicenter of their teachings, before centuries upon centuries of interpretation by humans and egregores? How are we all experiencing God’s love, compassion, joy, gratitude, and respect for all beings of the Universe?

The Dark Light of Location 4 leaves these questions for the reader.

## A: The Sacred Darkness of God
We find these beautiful paragraphs in *Psalms 91:1–8*,[^2]  helping us filter the darkness of truth from the lies of the Devil in both day and night:  
> ”He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty.  
>I will say of the LORD, *He is* my refuge and my fortress: my God; in him will I trust.  
>Surely he shall deliver thee from the snare of the fowler, *and* from the noisome pestilence.  
>He shall cover thee with his feathers, and under his wings shalt thou trust: his truth *shall be thy* shield and buckler.  
>Thou shalt not be afraid for the terror by night; *nor* for the arrow *that* flieth by day;  
>*Nor* for the pestilence *that* walketh in darkness; *nor* for the destruction *that* wasteth at noonday.  
>A thousand shall fall at thy side, and ten thousand at thy right hand; *but* it shall not come nigh thee.“

To what extent is Location 4 referenced by the ancient authors of the *Holy Bible* and other sacred writings, versus their words coincidentally aligning with Location 4’s properties? I have no clue, other than to state that Location 4 could be tremendously helpful for staying functional in violent environments, and that the *Old Testament* demonstrates many examples of nonduality in both beneficial and harmful forms.

The presence of God occurs everywhere and influences all of reality. If one doesn’t believe in God, then interlocking egregoric currents of positivity are nearly as pervasive in our daily experience.

I highly suspect the addictive properties of Location 4 play a role in many addictions to drugs, violence, gambling, sex, compulsive overeating, and other activities. Time and time again, I have heard fellow addicts describe the strange, empty satisfaction of feeling *nothing* during their addictions. And of losing control over their actions. These are properties of the Location 4 experience.

While demonic nonduality blesses harm, the Dark Light acknowledges brutality without glamorizing it.

Location 4 is equally useful at this moment for helping me stay away from drugs, because no matter what happens, there is the blue sky of existence. Every moment of the day is a different moment from the last! Every room brings a different sensory experience and a different set of thoughts and memories! Ah, the diversity of the Universe!

I *am* known to make incorrect assumptions about spirituality. More research is needed to sort out the effects of the Layers and Locations in shaping human behavior. Luckily, the shadow of the Almighty wishes for humanity to succeed in shaping our experiences toward understanding and integration!

## B: Jesus Declaring War on the Cult of Animal Sacrifice
Epiphanius quotes[^3] in *Panarion 30.14.15* the following statement from Jesus in what is now called the seventh fragment of the *Gospel of the Ebionites*:
>“I have come to abolish sacrifices and if you do not stop sacrificing the wrath will not cease from you”

There is a brutality and coldness in the words above. There is also compassion for the animals tortured and killed in religious rituals; and presumably for the religious fellows of Jesus, who would have invited many attack from evil spirits and egregores via the spiritual contamination of their behaviors drawing in evil and misfortune like a lighthouse at sea.

Dr. Jeffery Martin and Annemiek Meyer warn on page 48 of their groundbreaking work *The Layers of Depth in Fundamental Wellbeing*[^4] that specializing in Layer 4 can lead to amoral behavior. At the same time, Participant 49 (page 121) describes being unable to effectively utilize Layer 4 of Location 3 at first, until he returned to Location 3 from later Locations. 

Jesus Himself demonstrated an abnormal blend of compassion and detachment from social norms, allowing Him to follow what he felt were higher values from God. While I think Jesus traveled far beyond Location 3 or Location 4, He did leave us a great set of puzzle pieces for combining the light and darkness of truth, love, and virtue. Two opposite-color sides of the same coin.

The true enemies are the Devil and the egregores, demons, and ghosts under his sway; it is only the parasitic malice carried by humanity, not our core beingness.

## C: Hyperfluid Fundamental Wellbeing Uniting Locations Three and Four as Needed
The Center for the Study of Non-Symbolic Consciousness strongly promotes hyperfluid forms of Fundamental Wellbeing for maximizing both the richness and utility of daily life:[^6] 
> ”In addition, hyperfluidity in Fundamental Wellbeing can have many benefits. It means that the trade-offs that are normally involved in choosing a location to live one’s life are no longer relevant. Someone doesn’t have to think ‘well, I really want to live in Location 3, so I’ll just not run a business’, or ‘Location 4 is so amazing but I just can’t go there because it’ll wreck my relationship’, and so on.”

An illustration of the Dark Light of Location 4: the plain fact that incorporating Location 4 and Location 3 in the same system can maximize the benefits and alleviate the drawbacks of warmth and coldness alike. The Universe still exists, no matter what we say about it. Life exists even if I say that life is meaningless.

What are ways to cultivate hyperfluidity between Layers and Locations? In *The Finders*, Martin states:[^7]
 * **(Page 135):** We gain the forms of Fundamental Wellbeing we optimize our systems for.
 * **(Pages 200-201):** utilizing a wide range of Awakening techniques, while continually adjusting to focus on the most effective ones for yourself at a given time, makes it much more likely to shift Locations.

## D: The Teachings of Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva
What does the following passage from *The Flower Adornment Sutra* about the legendary Bodhisattva of Compassion have to do with the low-emotion interstellar vacuum of Location 4? At first glance, not much:[^8] 
>Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva was sitting there in the full-lotus posture on a vajra-jewel stone, surrounded by countless respectful bodhisattvas, all of whom sat there atop jewel stones as he taught them a Dharma discourse on the great kindness and the great compassion with which he encouraged them to devote themselves to gathering in all beings.  
>Having observed this, Sudhana was overcome with joyous exultation. He then pressed his palms together and gazed attentively, eyes unblinking, at what he beheld there. He then had these thoughts:  
>The good spiritual guides are the Tathāgata himself;  
>The good spiritual guides are the cloud of all dharmas;  
>The good spiritual guides are the treasury of all meritorious qualities;  
>The good spiritual guides are only rarely encountered;  
>The good spiritual guides are the cause of the jewels of the ten powers;  
>The good spiritual guides are the endlessly burning torch of wisdom;  
>The good spiritual guides are the roots and sprouts of merit;  
>The good spiritual guides are the guides on the ocean of wisdom; and
>The good spiritual guides are the provisions on the path to all-knowledge.

The all-directional love of Location 3, while invigorating and uplifting oneself and one’s sphere of influence, can be taken advantage of by demonic forces. Hence the advice in part C about hyperfluidity helping a person in Location 3 run a business successfully.

If one does not believe in demons as literal entities, then the interlocking currents of negativity in the world fill a similar role. It is good that there are so many positive currents of love, strength, and wisdom holding evil back from victory!

While I can’t give anyone a brain scan, especially not people from thousands of years ago, there is a supreme matter-of-factness behind the ornate prose of *The Flower Adornment Sutra*. Like a cybernetic exoskeleton protecting Avalokiteśvara’s essence from those who despise innocence and joy.

When I see Republicans and Democrats horrendously verbally abusing each other, while complaining about the other side verbally abusing them, the Dark Light of Location 4 tells me to have compassion for all of them, for they are not consciously aware of how to disentangle themselves from the cycle of outrage, mind-identification, and traumatic stress. 

The accursed Void Lantern of Location 4 tells me that everyone should teleport into a well-adapted hyperfluid form of Awakening / Fundamental Wellbeing, whereby most forms of emotional abuse are more of a mild smudge on the windshield than any genuine threat to mental health;

however, I *still* struggle disentangling myself from the loop of grievance and trolling, so I know *it just absolutely can’t be that simple for everyone to suddenly wake up with a more positive mindset, or they would already be doing it*.

In the Dark Light of Location 4, we recognize the simple fact that all humans are complex beings existing in the Universe!
