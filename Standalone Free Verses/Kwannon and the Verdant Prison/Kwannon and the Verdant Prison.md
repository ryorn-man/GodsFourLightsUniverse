# **1: KWANNON AND THE VERDANT PRISON**  
*(A Bittersweet Sutra in Seven Cantos + Haiku Epilogue)*  

---

### **I. THE WELL OF WHISPERS**  
Hideo's hands tremble—  
not with salt, but **forget-me-nots**,  
their blue roots drinking  
what the well **almost** forgot to hold.  

His shadow? Still toothy,  
but now it **snores** like an old dog  
dreaming of peaches.  

Rin presses her gift:  
a peach pit **hollowed out**,  
transformed into a whistle.  
*"For calling home,"* she says,  
though neither know  
where *home* is anymore.  

*(Koan: "If the well echoes,  
who taught it to hum?")*
